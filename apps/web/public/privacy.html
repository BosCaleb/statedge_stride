StatEdge Stride privacy notice

What we hold

For each athlete: first and last name, year of birth, an optional school or club number, consent records, and the results of running analyses (measurements, the movement landmarks found in the video, and coach notes). Videos stay on the coach's phone and are not uploaded.

For each coach: name, email address, role and when they last used the app. Coach email addresses and names are also held by the sign-in service, Microsoft Entra External ID, which stores them outside South Africa.

Why

To measure running technique for coaching, as a coaching aid. It is not a medical assessment.

How long

Results are kept until they or the athlete are deleted. Videos kept in the app on a phone are removed after the organisation's retention period. Shared report links expire after at most 90 days. Deleted data can remain in encrypted database backups until the backup retention period ends.

Who sees it

Coaches of the athlete's organisation. A report shared by link shows only the athlete's first name and last initial.

Your rights

Consent can be withdrawn at any time through the coach or organisation. An organisation administrator can export everything held about an athlete, or delete it all.
