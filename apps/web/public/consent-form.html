What is collected and why

The coach films the athlete running with a phone. The app measures running technique from the video on the phone, as a coaching aid. It is not a medical assessment and does not diagnose or predict injury. The video stays on the coach's phone. The results, the movement landmarks found in the video, and coach notes are stored by StatEdge.

How long it is kept, and who sees it

Results are kept until they or the athlete are deleted. Coaches of the organisation can see them. A report shared by link shows the athlete's first name and last initial only, and the link expires.

Withdrawing

Consent can be withdrawn at any time by telling the coach or organisation. All data about the athlete can be deleted on request.
